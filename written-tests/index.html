Written tests FAQ

Questions about quizzes & written tests

What question types do I get?
Exam-style MCQs, fill-in-the-blank, and written short-answer questions — all generated from the lecture you built.
How are my written answers marked?
MedBank compares what you wrote against the lecture's key points, gives you a score, and tells you what was strong and what you missed.
Is the marking exact?
It's a helpful guide to steer your revision, not an official examiner — use the feedback to spot gaps, and check anything important against your note.
Can I retry a question?
Yes — rewrite your answer and mark it again, or drill the questions you keep getting wrong.
Is it free?
You can take written tests on the lecture you build for free. Importing more lectures is a premium upgrade.
