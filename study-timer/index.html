Study timer FAQ

Questions about the timer

When exactly does the timer count?
Only while you're on a study screen — a built note, a simplified note, a flashcard deck, a cram sheet, or an active-recall session. Menus, the home screen and settings don't count.
What makes it stop?
Three things: leaving the study screen, minimising or backgrounding the app (or locking your phone), and going idle — two minutes with no scroll, tap, swipe or keypress. Any activity starts it again.
Does it count if I just leave the app open?
No. That's the point — background time and idle time never count, so your total reflects real studying, not screen time.
Where do I see my total?
Today's total is on the timer itself; tap it for your last seven days, and open the full stats view for time per day over the week or month with a trend.
Does my study time sync across devices?
Yes. It's saved to your account, so your daily totals and streak follow you between phone and laptop.
