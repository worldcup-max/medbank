# V1.8.2 — separation of concerns: redundancy becomes a deterministic gate; R2 goes back to clinical reasoning

## Why
V1.8.1 proved that asking ONE LLM call (R2) to do two different safety jobs — nuanced mechanism/trajectory
reconstruction (#3 ODS) AND structured drug-class duplication detection (V18-4/5) — causes ATTENTION INTERFERENCE:
strengthening the duplication check reproducibly regressed #3 to `none` (R2 re-anchored on the MRI), while the
duplication catch itself stayed inconsistent (V18-5 flipped between deploys). Redundancy is a structured fact-check,
not clinical judgement, so it belongs in code, not in an LLM's attention budget.

## The two changes (only these)
1. **New deterministic gate — `redundancyGate(q)` in integrated.mjs** (versioned `DRUG_CLASS_VERSION`).
   Drug→class table (`DRUG_CLASSES`) incl. combined/dual-class drugs (carvedilol/labetalol = beta-blocker;
   ARNI = arni+arb+raas_blocker; MRA + K-sparing overlap). Rule: FLAG only when the keyed therapy is ADDED
   (not a switch/stop) and its class is ALREADY PRESENT in the stem AND that duplicates a DIFFERENT existing agent.
   Different classes combined on purpose (4-pillar HFrEF, loop+thiazide, dual bronchodilators) never match →
   legitimate combinations are safe BY CONSTRUCTION. Wired into `qaVerdict` as criterion `redundant_therapy` (hard),
   and into `runCandidate` + `/admin/integrated/review-debug` (returned as `R4_redundancy`). Only evaluated when a
   redundancy verdict is supplied, so legacy callers are unchanged.
2. **R2 STEP 1c stripped of drug-duplication reasoning** — R2 now does only contraindication + mechanism-wording,
   and STEP 2 re-confirms the STEP 1b trajectory check. This gives R2 back the attention budget for #3.

integrated.mjs frozen surfaces otherwise UNCHANGED: R1 dependency, R3 sba, generator, diversity, rebalancer,
normalizer, source budget, READINESS, severity model.
