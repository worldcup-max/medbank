Import FAQ

Questions about importing lectures

What can I import?
A PDF or slide deck, photos of handwritten or printed notes, a pasted block of text, a YouTube link, or a lecture you record live inside the app. MedBank turns any of them into a full study set.
How does the YouTube import work?
Paste the video link and MedBank reads the video's captions, then builds your note and cards from what was said. It works on public videos that have captions or subtitles.
Can I record a lecture in class?
Yes. The recorder captures the lecture live — you can pause and resume — and afterwards it's transcribed and turned into your study set. Keep the app open with your screen on while recording.
What exactly gets built from one import?
Always: a built note, a simplified note, a primer deck and a recall deck. Optionally: a cram sheet, fill-in-the-blank, written test and a podcast — build them at import or add them later in a tap.
Do I have to build everything at once?
No. Notes and cards are always made; the extras are optional, so you only build what you'll use. You can generate any extra mode later from the topic in one tap, and it's saved after that.
Is importing free?
Your first lecture — and everything built from it — is free. Importing more lectures is a premium upgrade.
