Podcast FAQ

Questions about lecture podcasts

What does the episode cover?
The two hosts work through the actual lecture you built — the key concepts, the "why", and the exam-relevant points — as a natural back-and-forth conversation.
What are the voices like?
Two natural-sounding hosts by default. If you want an even richer sound, you can switch on premium voices in the host picker.
What is "raise your hand"?
A button that pauses the episode so you can ask a question. A tutor answers using your lecture, then you drop back into the conversation.
Can I download or play it offline?
Episodes are saved to your account so you can come back to them, and MedBank installs to your home screen like an app for easy access on the go.
Is it free?
You can generate an episode from the lecture you build for free. Importing more lectures is a premium upgrade.
