FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free to start?
Yes. You can build one full lecture and use everything on it — notes, flashcards, quizzes, the podcast, the AI tutor and active recall — free, forever. You only pay when you want to import more lectures.
How accurate are the AI-generated cards?
They're built strictly from your lecture, and they're good — but AI isn't perfect, and medicine demands accuracy. So every card is editable: fix it, regenerate it, or flag it in one tap.
Can I use my own lectures?
That's the whole point. Upload your slides or notes, paste a YouTube link, or record the class live — MedBank builds your study set from your material, not generic content.
Does it work on my phone / offline?
MedBank runs in your browser and installs to your home screen like an app. Your notes and cards work offline; building new content and the AI features need a connection.
Will it actually help for my exams?
It's built on active recall and spaced repetition — the two techniques with the strongest evidence for long-term memory — plus mock exams and written practice that mirror how you're really tested.
Is my data private?
Your imported lectures, notes and progress are tied to your own account and synced privately to it. Your content is yours.
