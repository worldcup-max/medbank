🎬 VisualizeBiology demo — 100 level
The MedBank Visualize engine on real 100-level Biology — same renderers as the app. New: the Punnett-square renderer, where the parents split into gametes, the gametes become the headers, the cells fill one at a time and the ratio is computed on screen. Alongside it: the membrane scene (water crossing in both directions), the graph engine on enzyme kinetics and population growth, four pathway flows, and the tree on classification.
