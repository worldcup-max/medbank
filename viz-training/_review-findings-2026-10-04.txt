STANDARDS GAP (section 4) - two rules proposed, neither written into the standards by this run. (1) A model that copies a constant from another model takes a DEPENDENCY on that file, and nothing in this loop re-runs a built item's harness when a model it depends on changes. This item sat `built` for fourteen hours carrying a stale copy while its own recorded proof said it passed, and only re-running the harness caught it. Propose for RENDER-STANDARD: when a model declares a copied constant, its harness must assert the copy against the SOURCE FILE's current value - a textual check that fails the moment the sibling changes - rather than against built geometry, which fails only once somebody happens to re-run it. (2) A measure whose name asserts a spatial relation must be rejected by a negative case placed outside that relation ON AN AXIS THE MEASURE DOES NOT READ. This item's acceptance battery has 21 negative cases and not one of them moves the tract laterally, which is why a z-only containment test shipped as a containment test.
