Real BodyParts3D mesh check

Opens the actual meshes from the CDN — the one thing the build container could not reach. This file inlines its scenes, so it works straight from file:// with no server.

What to judge — this is the product question, not the architecture question:
  • Does the anatomy look good enough to teach from?
  • When you tap a part, is the right thing lit — and is it obvious which one?
  • Cross-section, isolate and the camera views: useful, or fiddly?
  • Does "Follow the long head" (a degraded TRACE) actually read as following a course?
  • How does it feel on your phone — load time, frame rate, the parts list?
