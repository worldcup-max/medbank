Derive landmarks

Paste or drop a v2 scene. Every muscle in it is asked where it attaches, and the answer becomes a landmark.

Why this is not guesswork. A landmark like the supraglenoid tubercle has no model of its own — but the muscle that attaches there does. The nearest point on the bone to the end of that muscle is the landmark. Each row below reports the gap in millimetres: a few mm is tendon thickness, a large gap means the pairing is wrong and should be dropped rather than shipped.
