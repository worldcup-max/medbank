# Prompt sheet: severe malaria, set 7 (m-child-gh-07)

**Patient:** Chidi, an 11-year-old Igbo boy in a school uniform. **Hospital:** a state specialist hospital in the south-east, with a big children's ward of 1990s design, cream and maroon colours, high ceilings and louvre windows.
**What this set teaches:** severe malaria with jaundice, repeated vomiting and low blood sugar (sweaty, shaky, confused). The skin and abdomen close-ups carry the signs.

## How to use this sheet

1. In the Flow project "Nov 17 - 09:26", make **base** first with the prompt box on the main project page.
2. For every **edit**, click **base** in Flow first (for the flipbook frames, click **distress**), then paste the prompt into the edit box.
3. For every **new picture** (the arrival and the close-ups), attach **base** as the reference, then paste the prompt.
4. Download each picture and rename it to exactly its name in bold, for example `exam-eyes.png`. Put them all in one folder and tell me, or upload them in the thread.
5. If Flow refuses a prompt, skip that picture and tell me which one. I'll reword it.

### base (new, main prompt box)
```
Make only ONE image. Wide 16:9 illustration for a medical training game, painterly semi-realistic storybook style, soft ink lines and watercolour texture, bright late-morning light through louvres. Side view of one bed space in a large Nigerian state specialist hospital children's ward: high ceiling, cream walls with a maroon lower band, tall glass louvre windows, a ceiling fan, a white metal bedside locker with a flask and a plastic cup, a folding screen on wheels with faded maroon fabric, a second empty bed far in the background, a speckled terrazzo floor. An 11-year-old Nigerian Igbo boy (dark brown skin, short black hair, thin face, a white school shirt and khaki shorts) lies on his back on a standard white metal hospital bed with a blue sheet, head on the LEFT of the frame, feet to the right. He looks unwell and tired: sweaty forehead, eyes half closed, lips dry. An empty IV stand by the head of the bed, a switched-off monitor on a trolley at the RIGHT. No other people, no text, no logos. Camera at bed height, whole bed visible.
```
