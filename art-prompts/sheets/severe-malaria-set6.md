# Prompt sheet: severe malaria, set 6 (m-child-phc-06)

**Patient:** Ebi, a 3-year-old Ijaw boy in a green football jersey. **Hospital:** a riverine health post in the Niger Delta, a wooden building on stilts over the creek.
**What this set teaches:** cerebral malaria, where the child is hard to wake and restless and confused, with a high fever. The abdomen shows a big spleen.

## How to use this sheet

1. In the Flow project "Nov 17 - 09:26", make **base** first with the prompt box on the main project page.
2. For every **edit**, click **base** in Flow first (for the flipbook frames, click **distress**), then paste the prompt into the edit box.
3. For every **new picture** (the arrival and the close-ups), attach **base** as the reference, then paste the prompt.
4. Download each picture and rename it to exactly its name in bold, for example `exam-eyes.png`. Put them all in one folder and tell me, or upload them in the thread.
5. If Flow refuses a prompt, skip that picture and tell me which one. I'll reword it.

### base (new, main prompt box)
```
Make only ONE image. Wide 16:9 illustration for a medical training game, painterly semi-realistic storybook style, soft ink lines and watercolour texture, humid hazy morning light. Side view of the small treatment room of a riverine health post in the Niger Delta, a wooden building raised on stilts over a creek: walls of painted wooden planks in faded turquoise, a wide window with a wooden shutter open onto a brown creek with a long wooden canoe and mangrove trees, a plastic water drum in the corner, a small wooden shelf with plastic medicine boxes, a ceiling fan that is not turning. A 3-year-old Nigerian Ijaw boy (dark brown skin, short black hair, chubby cheeks, a green football jersey and red shorts) lies on his back on a low metal bed with a thin mattress and a faded floral bedsheet, head on the LEFT of the frame, feet to the right. He looks hot and unwell: flushed face, sweaty, half-closed eyes. An empty IV stand by the head of the bed, a switched-off small monitor on a plastic stool at the RIGHT. No other people, no text, no logos. Camera at bed height, whole bed visible.
```
