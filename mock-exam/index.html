Mock exam FAQ

Questions about mock exams

Where do the questions come from?
From the lectures you've built — MedBank pulls exam-style MCQs from your own material, so the mock tests what you're actually studying.
Can I choose the length?
Yes — pick a scope (one subject or everything) and 10, 20, 40 or 60 questions, with an estimated time for each.
Do I get feedback during the exam?
No — that's deliberate. Like the real paper, you only see results after you submit, so you practise committing to answers under time.
What happens to my mistakes?
They're collected into your mistakes and hard cards so you can drill exactly what you got wrong afterwards.
Is it free?
You can sit a mock built from your free lecture. Importing more lectures for a bigger question bank is a premium upgrade.
