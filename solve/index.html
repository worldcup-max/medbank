Snap & solve FAQ

Questions about Snap & solve

What can I snap?
An MCQ, a written past question, or a diagram. You can also type the question in or add context to help.
Do I just get the answer?
No — you get the reasoning worked out step by step, including why the wrong options are wrong, so you learn how to approach similar questions.
Is it always correct?
It's a strong study aid, but AI can make mistakes and medicine needs accuracy — treat it as a worked example to learn from and verify anything important.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — snapping a photo is easiest on your phone, and MedBank installs to your home screen like an app.
Is it free?
Snap & solve is available to signed-in students. Importing more lectures for your full study set is a premium upgrade.
