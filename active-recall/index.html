Active recall FAQ

Questions about recall & scheduling

What is active recall?
Instead of re-reading notes, you try to answer a question from memory. The effort of retrieving the answer is what builds strong, lasting memory — and it's how MedBank's recall decks work.
How does the scheduling work?
Each card climbs a ladder as you get it right — back after 1 day, then 3 days, then a week, then two weeks, then long-term. A miss drops it to the start, so you see struggling cards sooner and solid ones less often.
Do I have to decide what to study?
No. Open Active Recall and MedBank has already gathered the cards that are due, overdue or giving you trouble across all your lectures.
Can I focus on just my weak cards?
Yes — filter a session to only the cards you keep missing, your hard/starred cards, or this week's mistakes.
Do I need to study every day?
Little and often works best, and a streak helps keep the habit — but the schedule adapts to when you actually review, so you won't lose your progress if you skip a day.
