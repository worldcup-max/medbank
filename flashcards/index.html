Flashcard FAQ

Questions about the flashcard maker

What can I make flashcards from?
PDFs, slides, Word docs, photos of handwritten notes, a pasted block of text, a YouTube link, or a lecture you record live in the app. MedBank pulls out the key terms and concepts and turns them into cards.
Can I edit the cards after they're made?
Every card is fully editable — reword the question, fix the answer, regenerate it from your note, or flag one as wrong to hide it. The AI deck is a starting point, not a locked set.
Do the cards use spaced repetition?
Yes. Each card is tracked as Unfamiliar, Learning, Familiar or Mastered, and MedBank schedules reviews so cards come back right before you'd forget them. You can also filter a deck to just your weak cards.
Does it work for any subject?
The cards come from whatever you upload, so it works for any course you have material for — though MedBank is tuned for medical and health-science content and exams.
Is it free?
You can build your first lecture's deck — and use everything on it — for free. Importing more lectures is a premium upgrade.
Can I study my cards on my phone?
Yes. MedBank installs to your home screen like an app, and your decks and progress sync to your account across devices.
