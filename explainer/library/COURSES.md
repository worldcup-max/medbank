# MedBank plate library: course line-up (Nigerian MBBS)

Build order: one course at a time, top 50 topics (wave 1) first, then the rest by priority.

| # | Course | Level | Core textbook for the topic list | Fit for model-driven plates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Physiology** | 200–300L (MB1) | Guyton & Hall, 15th ed (2025) | Excellent: almost every topic is a mechanism with numbers |
| 2 | Medical Biochemistry | 200–300L (MB1) | Lippincott Illustrated Reviews: Biochemistry; Harper's | Very good: pathways, enzyme kinetics, regulation |
| 3 | Anatomy (gross, histology, embryology, neuroanatomy) | 200–300L (MB1) | Snell's Clinical Anatomy by Regions; Langman's Embryology; Junqueira's Histology | Good for structure/embryology sequences; overlaps the 3D model library |
| 4 | Pharmacology & Therapeutics | 400L | Katzung; Rang & Dale | Very good: receptors, dose-response, PK curves |
| 5 | Histopathology (Morbid Anatomy) | 400L | Robbins Basic Pathology | Good: inflammation, neoplasia, haemodynamic disorders |
| 6 | Chemical Pathology | 400L | Marshall's Clinical Chemistry | Good: acid-base, electrolytes, endocrine tests |
| 7 | Haematology & Blood Transfusion | 400L | Hoffbrand's Essential Haematology | Good: haemopoiesis, coagulation, anaemias |
| 8 | Medical Microbiology & Parasitology | 400L | Jawetz; Cheesbrough (parasitology) | Mixed: life cycles and pathogenesis yes, classification lists no |
| 9 | Community Medicine | 400–600L | Park's Preventive and Social Medicine | Low: epidemiology curves and outbreak models only |
| 10 | Paediatrics | 500L | Nelson Essentials of Pediatrics | Moderate: neonatal physiology, fluids, growth |
| 11 | Obstetrics & Gynaecology | 500L | Ten Teachers (O&G) | Moderate: labour, menstrual cycle, fetal monitoring |
| 12 | Psychiatry | 500L | Oxford Shorter Textbook of Psychiatry | Low: neurotransmitter mechanisms only |
| 13 | Internal Medicine | 600L | Davidson's Principles & Practice of Medicine | Good: disease physiology (heart failure, DKA, AKI) |
| 14 | Surgery | 600L | Bailey & Love | Moderate: shock, fluids, wound healing |
| 15 | Minor postings (ENT, Ophthalmology, Dermatology, Radiology, Anaesthesia) | 500–600L | Respective short texts | Selected topics only |

Pre-medical (100L): Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Maths can reuse the same engine (the SN2 tutor is already built).
